Self-Assessment

During my more than 15-week internship at Amazon Web Services Viet Nam Company Limited under the First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ 2026) program from May 25, 2026 to September 25, 2026, under the dedicated mentorship of Mr. Nguyen Gia Hung, Mr. Lu Hoan Thien, and the program mentors, I truly had the opportunity to apply the theoretical knowledge acquired from university into an international-standard enterprise environment. The academic and technical resources provided by the company helped me accomplish the research and deployment of a real-world project. In addition, the weekly events organized by the company were deeply meaningful to me, offering invaluable opportunities to absorb insights and learn from experienced guest speakers; every time I attended an event, I was genuinely thrilled because it was a chance to draw inspiration and knowledge to apply directly to my project. I participated in a total of 17 sessions comprising 5 event sessions and 12 office sessions, and published 5 in-depth technical blogs on AWS Study Group. Throughout this tenure, I consistently upheld professional discipline, actively pursued learning, and integrated mentor feedback to drive excellence.

Acquired Knowledge & Professional Progress

Throughout my internship at Amazon Web Services Viet Nam Company Limited, I acquired substantial knowledge and honed key competencies across two main dimensions:

First, regarding technical expertise and practical cloud engineering skills, I mastered the end-to-end design, deployment, and operation of core services across the AWS platform. I gained proficiency in architecting secure Amazon VPC network environments with public and private subnets, provisioning and managing Amazon EC2 compute instances, optimizing storage and file pipelines on Amazon S3 via Pre-signed URLs, and integrating Amazon RDS relational databases alongside high-performance Amazon ElastiCache Redis caching clusters. Furthermore, I developed multi-layered defense using AWS IAM least privilege policies, ACM SSL/TLS certificates, AWS WAF application firewalls, and centralized observability telemetry through Amazon CloudWatch.

Second, regarding engineering mindset and professional methodology, I began applying the principles of the AWS Well-Architected Framework to optimize performance and operational expenditure. The internship substantially honed my ability to digest in-depth English technical documentation, reinforced reflective learning by authoring 5 technical blogs on AWS Study Group, and fostered professional discipline, constructive inquiry, and confidence when discussing technical solutions with mentors.

Areas for Future Improvement

To sustain personal growth on the path toward becoming a professional cloud engineer, I recognize several key areas requiring further dedication:

  • Independent Architectural Design Capabilities: When faced with an actual business scenario requiring independent requirement analysis and proposing a cloud architecture from scratch, I still experience hesitation and need to build a more rigorous end-to-end design methodology.
  • Transitioning to Infrastructure as Code (IaC): Although familiar with CloudFormation concepts, I still relied frequently on manual configuration through the AWS Management Console; I need to establish disciplined, code-first automation habits for production workloads.
  • Advanced Networking & Distributed Systems: Having established a solid grasp of fundamental VPC configurations, I recognize the need to further explore advanced hybrid networking patterns, transit gateways, and large-scale multi-tier architectures.

Overall Conclusion

Summarizing the entire internship journey at AWS, I believe I have acquired an extensive foundation of both theoretical principles and hands-on cloud engineering skills. Technologies and services that I previously only knew by name have now become familiar through hands-on practice, helping me understand their operational mechanics and apply my knowledge directly in real-world scenarios. This internship has notably enhanced my ability to comprehend academic and technical documentation in English, as well as my proactiveness in communicating with the AWS support team via support cases to overcome account limits and multi-step verifications. I hope that the fundamental knowledge and hands-on experience gained at the company will empower my future career development in Cloud Computing, enabling me to solve complex engineering challenges that I was previously unable to address.

I express my sincere gratitude to Mr. Nguyen Gia Hung, Mr. Lu Hoan Thien, and the mentors at Amazon Web Services Viet Nam Company Limited, the FCAJ program coordinators, and the faculty of the Faculty of Information Technology at the Industrial University of Ho Chi Minh City (IUH) for their continuous guidance, support, and mentorship throughout my internship journey.
